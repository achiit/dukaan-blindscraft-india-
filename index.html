Questions we hear often

Do motorized tracks need rewiring?

Not usually. Most run from a nearby socket, and we can hide the cable in the pelmet. For new builds, we advise a power point at the curtain corner.

Will it work during a power cut?

Yes. Every motor can be pulled by hand, and battery models keep working through outages.

Can you fit blinds in a rented flat?

Yes. We offer no-drill and clip-on options that leave no marks when you move out.

Do you work outside the big cities?

Yes. Send us your window sizes and photos. We dispatch across India and arrange a fitter or a video-guided install.

How much does it cost?

It depends on window size, fabric and motor type. The site visit and quote are free, so you can compare before you decide.
